# Central High FBLA Wins State Championship in Business Plan Competition

**March 15, 2024** - The Central High School FBLA chapter made history this weekend by winning first place in the Business Plan competitive event at the State Leadership Conference held in the state capital.

## The Winning Innovation

Seniors Maria Rodriguez and James Chen presented "EcoPack Solutions," a comprehensive business plan for a company that produces biodegradable packaging materials from agricultural waste. Their innovative approach to sustainable packaging caught the attention of judges and fellow competitors alike.

"We saw a real problem in our community with plastic waste, and we wanted to create a business solution that could make a difference," said Maria Rodriguez, chapter president. "This win validates that young entrepreneurs can develop viable solutions to environmental challenges."

## Competition Highlights

The team competed against 47 other chapters from across the state, presenting their business plan to a panel of industry professionals and educators. The competition required:

- A comprehensive written business plan
- A 10-minute presentation to judges
- A Q&A session demonstrating deep understanding of their business model
- Financial projections and market analysis

## Road to Nationals

This victory qualifies the team to compete at the National Leadership Conference this summer in Atlanta, Georgia. The chapter has been preparing for months under the guidance of advisor Mrs. Jennifer Martinez.

"I'm incredibly proud of Maria and James," said Mrs. Martinez. "Their dedication to research, their innovative thinking, and their presentation skills exemplify what FBLA is all about - developing the next generation of business leaders."

## Chapter Success

This win adds to Central High FBLA's impressive track record this year:

- 12 students qualified for state competition
- 8 top-10 finishes across various events
- 3 first-place wins at the regional level
- Over $15,000 in scholarship opportunities earned by members

## About Central High FBLA

The Central High School FBLA chapter, established in 2010, has grown to over 120 active members. The chapter focuses on developing business skills, leadership abilities, and community engagement among high school students.
