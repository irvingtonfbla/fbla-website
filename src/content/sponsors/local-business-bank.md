# Local Business Bank Partnership

Local Business Bank has been a cornerstone supporter of our FBLA chapter since 2020, providing invaluable resources and opportunities for our members to develop real-world business and financial skills.

## Partnership Benefits

### Financial Education Workshops
- Monthly workshops on personal finance, business banking, and entrepreneurship
- Guest speakers from the banking industry
- Hands-on experience with business financial planning

### Scholarship Program
- Annual $2,500 scholarship for outstanding FBLA members
- Merit-based selection focusing on leadership and business acumen
- Additional micro-scholarships for competition participation

### Internship Opportunities
- Summer internship program for junior and senior members
- Exposure to various banking departments
- Mentorship from experienced banking professionals

### Competition Support
- Sponsorship of travel expenses for state and national competitions
- Funding for chapter materials and resources
- Professional development opportunities

## Community Impact

Local Business Bank's support has enabled our chapter to:
- Send 15+ members to state competitions annually
- Provide financial literacy education to over 200 students
- Establish a chapter emergency fund for member support
- Create lasting partnerships with local business community

## Recognition

"Local Business Bank's commitment to developing future business leaders aligns perfectly with FBLA's mission. Their support has been instrumental in our chapter's success." - Ms. Jennifer Thompson, Chapter Advisor
