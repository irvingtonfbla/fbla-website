# Regional Leadership Conference 2024

**April 12-14, 2024** | Downtown Convention Center

The Regional Leadership Conference is one of the most important events of the FBLA year, bringing together over 1,500 students from across our region to compete, learn, and network.

## Event Schedule

### Friday, April 12
- **2:00 PM** - Registration and Check-in
- **3:00 PM** - Opening General Session
- **4:30 PM** - Competitive Events Round 1
- **6:00 PM** - Welcome Reception
- **7:30 PM** - Regional Awards Ceremony

### Saturday, April 13
- **8:00 AM** - Breakfast and Networking
- **9:00 AM** - Competitive Events Round 2
- **11:00 AM** - Workshop Sessions
- **12:30 PM** - Lunch with Guest Speaker
- **2:00 PM** - Final Competitive Events
- **4:00 PM** - State Qualifier Announcements
- **6:00 PM** - Regional Banquet

### Sunday, April 14
- **9:00 AM** - Leadership Workshops
- **11:00 AM** - Community Service Project
- **1:00 PM** - Closing Ceremony
- **2:30 PM** - Departure

## Competitive Events

Students can compete in over 50 different business-related events, including:

**Business Communication**
- Business Communication
- Public Speaking
- Impromptu Speaking
- Parliamentary Procedure

**Business Administration**
- Business Law
- Business Ethics
- Human Resource Management
- International Business

**Accounting & Finance**
- Accounting I & II
- Banking & Financial Systems
- Securities & Investments
- Personal Finance

**Marketing**
- Advertising
- Digital Marketing
- Sales Presentation
- Sports & Entertainment Marketing

**Technology**
- Cybersecurity
- Network Design
- Website Design
- Database Design & Applications

**Entrepreneurship**
- Business Plan
- Entrepreneurship
- Innovation Plan
- Start-up Entrepreneur

## Workshop Sessions

Choose from engaging workshops led by industry professionals:

- **"Building Your Personal Brand"** - Social media strategist Sarah Johnson
- **"Financial Literacy for Teens"** - Local bank president Michael Chen
- **"Entrepreneurship in the Digital Age"** - Tech startup founder Lisa Martinez
- **"Leadership in Crisis"** - Former Fortune 500 CEO David Thompson
- **"Sustainable Business Practices"** - Environmental consultant Dr. Amanda Green

## Registration Information

**Cost:** $125 per student
**Includes:**
- All competitive event materials
- Workshop sessions
- Friday welcome reception
- Saturday lunch and banquet
- Sunday breakfast
- Conference t-shirt and materials

**Registration Deadline:** March 25, 2024

**What to Bring:**
- Business professional attire
- Laptop/tablet for some competitive events
- Notebook and pens
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Positive attitude and school spirit!

## Transportation & Lodging

**Transportation:** The school will provide bus transportation departing Friday at 12:00 PM and returning Sunday at 5:00 PM.

**Lodging:** Students will stay at the Conference Hotel (walking distance to venue) in supervised rooms with chaperones.

## Preparation

To prepare for competitive events:
1. Review event guidelines on the FBLA website
2. Attend our preparation workshops (Tuesdays after school)
3. Practice with teammates and get feedback
4. Study current business trends and news
