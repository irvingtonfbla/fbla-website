What is CTE?

Stands for: Career and Technical Education

CTE courses provide hands-on learning experiences that prepare students for high-skill, high-wage careers while building a strong foundation for college success.

With CTE, you can:

  • Ask any officer if your schedule has a FBLA approved CTE course!
  • Attend and compete at conferences such as Bay Section, States, and Nationals!
  • Gain real-world experience in your chosen field
  • Build professional networks and connections
